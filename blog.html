1. Why Does a Small Business Need a Website?

A website gives a small business a central place to explain what it offers, who it serves and how customers can get in touch. Potential customers can find key information without needing to send a message first.

Key benefits of a business website

  • Business credibility: Present your services, experience and contact information professionally.
  • Online visibility: Search engines can discover useful pages that answer customer questions.
  • Customer enquiries: Add contact details, enquiry forms or appointment links.
  • Information anytime: Customers can review your services outside normal business hours.

Start with a clear homepage, services page, about section and contact page. Make sure the site works well on mobile phones and provides accurate business information.

2. Website vs Social Media: Which Is Better for Your Business?

Social media helps businesses share updates and interact with followers. A website offers more control over how services, pricing, case studies and contact information are organised.

Social platforms can change their rules and reach, so relying on a single platform may limit your options. A website gives you a central destination to link from social profiles, advertisements and email campaigns.

A practical approach

  • Use social media to share updates and connect with your audience.
  • Use your website to explain services in detail and collect enquiries.
  • Keep your business name, contact details and service information consistent.

For many small businesses, using both together is more practical than choosing only one.

3. How Much Does a Business Website Cost in India?

Website pricing varies according to design complexity, number of pages, functionality and who builds and maintains the site. There is no single price that fits every business.

What affects the cost?

  • Design: A custom design may require more work than a simple template.
  • Features: Booking systems, online payments, user accounts and dashboards add complexity.
  • Domain and hosting: These may have recurring fees depending on the provider and plan.
  • Maintenance: Updates, backups, security and technical support may create ongoing costs.

Before requesting a quote, list the pages and features you need. Ask providers what is included, what recurring fees apply and who owns the website and domain. Compare proposals based on scope, not just the headline price.

4. How Can a Small Business Get More Customers Online?

Start by making it easy for potential customers to understand your offer and contact you. Online visibility usually takes consistent work; results are not guaranteed overnight.

Practical steps to try

  • Create useful website pages for your actual services and the questions customers ask.
  • Use descriptive page titles and clear headings that accurately match each page.
  • If eligible, create or maintain a Google Business Profile with accurate details.
  • Make your phone number, email and enquiry process easy to find.
  • Track website visits and enquiries to learn which channels are useful.

Avoid fake reviews, misleading claims and mass spam. Focus on helpful information and genuine relationships with potential customers.
