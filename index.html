GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter website typically takes 5–7 business days. Business websites may take 1–3 weeks, depending on the features and project requirements.

Is domain and hosting included in the price?

Domain registration and paid hosting are not included unless specifically mentioned in your proposal. We will explain any additional costs before starting.

How does the payment process work?

We agree on the scope, price and timeline before starting. Typically, 50% is paid upfront and the remaining 50% after completion, before final handover.

Can I request changes to my website?

Yes. Revisions will be agreed upon in advance. Additional features or changes outside the agreed scope may require an extra charge.

Do you provide maintenance and support?

Yes. We can provide maintenance and technical support based on your requirements. Any ongoing charges will be confirmed before the service begins.

Can you build websites for international clients?

Yes. Vyrqenta Technologies welcomes project enquiries from India and international clients, including the USA.
